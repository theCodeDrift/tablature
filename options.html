Tablature

Set up Chrome for Tablature

  1. Turn off Chrome's own tab group sync. Chrome saves every tab group and syncs it to your other computers, which brings back copies of groups Tablature has merged. Tablature shares groups its own way, so Chrome's isn't needed. In sync settings, choose Customize sync and turn off Saved tab groups (called Tab groups in some versions).

    Chrome doesn't let extensions read this setting, so Tablature can't check it for you.

  2. Clear out old copies. If saved groups have piled up on your bookmarks bar, right-click each extra one and choose Delete group or Unpin. You only need to do this once.
  3. Add your groups and rules below. Tabs that are already open are sorted as soon as a rule is added.

    Rules

    The first rule that matches a tab decides its group. Drag to reorder.

      How patterns work
      github.comgithub.com and www.github.com, any protocol. With subpages, every page on them.
      github.com/jakoboThat page. With subpages, also github.com/jakobo/anything, never github.com/jakobonew.
      www.github.comOnly the www address.
      *.bsky.appbsky.app and every subdomain, like staging.bsky.app.
      https://x.comOnly https.
      x.com/*/status* matches anything, including /.

      The part after ? or # in a tab's address is ignored.

      Behavior

      Merge same-name groups

      A tab you drag out of its group by hand stays where you put it until it closes, or until you forget manual moves.

      This machine

      Tablature shares each named group's tabs with your other computers through Chrome sync. The toolbar button offers to open the ones you don't have here. Nothing opens or closes on its own.
